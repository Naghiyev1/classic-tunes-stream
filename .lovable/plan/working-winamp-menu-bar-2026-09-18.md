# Working WinAmp Menu Bar

## Build
- Replace the decorative File, Play, Options, View, and Help labels with compact dropdown menus matching the active skin.
- Connect menu actions to existing player controls: station selection, favourites, play/pause, stop, previous/next, volume, equalizer, playlist, and color themes.
- Add small useful actions for resetting the equalizer and opening the free radio directory/about information.
- Close menus after selection, when clicking outside, and with Escape; preserve keyboard focus and phone usability.

## Validation
- Check every menu opens without changing the player dimensions.
- Verify actions work, menus close correctly, and the menu bar stays contained on a narrow phone screen.
- Confirm the app builds without errors.
